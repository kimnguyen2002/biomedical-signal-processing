# Books and References

## Main textbooks

The books below form the main reading foundation for these notes. I use them to study the theory, connect mathematical methods to physiological measurements, and place the ECG examples in context. Individual chapters also cite papers, software, and public datasets where appropriate.

| Book | Authors or editors | How it supports the material |
|---|---|---|
| *Digital Signal Processing: Principles, Algorithms, and Applications*, 4th ed. (2007) | John G. Proakis and Dimitris G. Manolakis | Discrete-time systems, Fourier analysis, sampling, and filtering |
| *Biomedical Signals and Systems: Acquisition, Processing, and Clinical Insights* (2026) | Almir Badnjević and Lemana Spahić | Measurement, acquisition, and biomedical applications |
| *ECG Signal Processing, Classification and Interpretation: A Comprehensive Framework of Computational Intelligence* (2012) | Adam Gacek and Witold Pedrycz, editors | ECG processing and computational analysis |
| *Feature Engineering and Computational Intelligence in ECG Monitoring* (2020) | Chengyu Liu and Jianqing Li, editors | ECG features and monitoring methods |
| *The ECG Made Easy*, 8th ed. (2013) | John R. Hampton | ECG waveforms and clinical interpretation |
| *Learn ECG in a Day: A Systematic Approach* (2013) | M. Sajjan | A systematic introduction to ECG interpretation |
